- Purchasing plans are rebuilt from the current Designer BOM whenever they are opened, saved, released, or synchronized.

- A single-BOM Ordering Sheet is rebuilt from the current purchasing plan, so removed BOM products no longer remain on the order.
- Combined Job Ordering Sheets are rebuilt from all current released BOM plans and no longer retain obsolete lines.
- Existing vendor assignments, selections, notes, and receiving quantities are preserved for matching products.
- The combined purchasing release action now uses an idempotent release path and confirms that the Ordering Sheet was created.
- Legacy per-BOM purchasing release is also routed through the safer order creation path.
- BOM changes made after purchasing has started continue to update the active Ordering Sheet.
- Existing shop-data.json, history, and shop-drawings are not included or changed.
