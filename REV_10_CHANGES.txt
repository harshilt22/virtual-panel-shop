ShadComm Virtual Shop REV 10

Purchaser released-BOM management added.

- Purchaser can open an Approved/Released BOM with "Manage Released BOM".
- Designer BOM quantities remain locked and unchanged.
- Purchaser can assign Shop Supply quantities per BOM line.
- Shop Supply reduces physical inventory and creates Job Materials allocations for Receiver.
- Purchaser can add purchasing-only extras from the Digital Library.
- Purchasing extras do not alter the Designer's released BOM.
- Remaining to Order = Required Qty - Shop Supply.
- Release to Ordering sends only Remaining to Order quantities to vendor orders.
- If everything is covered by shop stock, no vendor PO is created.
- Same Job Number continues through BOM, Shop Materials, Ordering, and Receiving.
