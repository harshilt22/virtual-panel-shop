JOB EXPLORER
- Added Jobs to the left navigation for all operational roles.
- Search by Job Number, project/name, Quote number, BOM number, or PO/order number.

  2. BOMs
  3. Ordering Sheets
  4. Receiving Report

- Receiving Report shows vendor, PO, part number, ordered, received, balance, and receiving notes.
- Receiving Report can be exported to CSV by Job Number.
- Existing Quote/BOM/Ordering records remain unchanged; this is a job-centered explorer view.
