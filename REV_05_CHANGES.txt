ShadComm Virtual Shop - REV 05

1. Website renamed to ShadComm Virtual Shop.
2. Estimator selecting a product from Shop Inventory immediately reserves that quantity for the Quote Job Number.
3. Reserved quantity is deducted from available Shop Inventory.
4. Removing/reducing an Inventory quote item returns the reserved quantity to Shop Inventory.
5. Added shared Job Materials / Shop Allocations records.
6. Receiver can see Job Number, product, allocated quantity and shop location.
7. Receiver can update material location, notes and status:
   - Allocated - Awaiting Shop Placement
   - Placed in Job Materials
   - Issued to Panel Build
8. Buyer can view Job Materials allocations.
9. Estimator Job Status shows allocated shop materials and Receiver placement status.
10. Existing shop-data.json files are automatically upgraded with an allocations section.
