SHADCOMM VIRTUAL SHOP - REV 06

Workflow correction:
1. Estimator quotes ONLY from the Digital Library.
2. Designer builds/updates BOM ONLY from the Digital Library.
3. Estimator/Designer do NOT reserve or consume physical shop inventory.
4. Approved BOM transfers to Buyer Ordering using the same Job Number.
5. Buyer uses "Check Shop Stock" on each ordering sheet.
6. Buyer can assign part of each BOM quantity from physical shop inventory.
7. Shop-assigned quantity is deducted from inventory and creates a Job Materials allocation for Receiver.
8. Remaining to Order = BOM Qty - From Shop.
9. The original BOM quantity is NEVER reduced or deleted by shop-stock allocation.
10. Receiver sees Buyer allocations in Job Materials and can mark physical location/status.
11. Vendor receiving applies only to Remaining to Order quantities.

Example:
BOM requires 10 contactors.
Buyer finds 4 in shop.
BOM remains 10.
From Shop = 4.
Remaining to Order = 6.
Receiver sees the 4 shop contactors allocated to that Job Number.
