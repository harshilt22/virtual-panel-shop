ShadComm Virtual Shop - REV 40
- Removed identical-panel quantity multiplier from Designer BOM.
- Designer BOM quantities always remain the quantities shown on the drawing / one panel design.
- Added Number of Identical Panels multiplier to Purchaser / Ordering Plan only.
- Purchasing Required Qty = BOM Qty per Panel x Panel Quantity.
- Shop Supplies and From Shop Inventory are deducted from the multiplied purchasing total; the remainder becomes vendor Order Qty.
- Purchasing Extras remain independently editable and are not automatically multiplied.
- Ordering sheet stores the panel multiplier and BOM Qty / Panel for traceability.
- Ordering/Vendor Excel reports now show BOM Qty / Panel, Panel Quantity, Purchasing Required, and Order Qty.
- Existing REV 39 features are preserved.
